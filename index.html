    Before you fly

    Travel rules, in plain words

    Rules change, so check the airline and destination before you travel. Here is what applies to our boxes today.

    Leaving Canada

    Syrup, butter and jelly
    Airport security treats anything you can pour or spread as a liquid. Each container must be 100 ml or less, and all of them must fit in one 1 L clear resealable bag.
    Candies, lollipops, popcorn
    Solids. No limit in your carry-on.
    Maple sugar
    Counts as a powder. Our 25 g jar is well under the limit.
    Checked bags
    No liquid limit. Wrap glass in clothing.
    Arriving inWhat to know
    United StatesSealed foods are generally allowed. Declare them at the border.
    United KingdomSyrup and sweets are allowed. Meat and dairy products are not.
    IndiaGifts count toward your duty-free allowance. A gift box is well within it.
    PakistanPersonal gifts are fine. Avoid amounts that look like goods for resale.
    UAEBring factory-sealed, labelled food only. Home-made food is not allowed.
    PhilippinesPackaged pasalubong is welcome. Keep the original labels visible.
    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it real maple syrup?

    Yes. The syrup is 100% pure Canadian maple syrup, Canada Grade A. The only ingredient is maple syrup.

    Can you ship outside Hamilton and the GTA?

    You can pick up in Hamilton, get GTA delivery, or have it shipped anywhere in Canada by Canada Post (the price shows at checkout). For shipping abroad, a bulk order or a special date, contact us. We will work out the best option for you and quote it before you pay.

    When can I pick up my order?

    From December 1. We will email you the pickup address and available times after you order.

    Can I change or cancel a pre-order?

    Yes. Cancel within 48 hours of placing your order for a full refund. After 48 hours, we refund your order minus a $10 cancellation fee. To change or cancel, email or call us with your order number.

    How many boxes can I take in one carry-on?

    Every liquid in our boxes is 100 ml or less, but they all share your one 1 L clear bag. One Carry-On Box or up to four Little Taste boxes fit comfortably. Put the rest in your checked bag.
